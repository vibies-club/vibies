# Vibies Community Rules

Vibies is a private club where beginners build real products together.
These rules keep it safe and useful for everyone — including our youngest members.

## Who we are

- We are builders. Everyone here makes things and shows them.
- We use **nicknames**, not full names.
- No photos, phone numbers, or addresses — yours or anyone else's.

## How we give feedback

- Feedback is about **the work, never the person**.
- Every piece of feedback names one thing to **keep** and one thing to **improve**.
- "This is bad" is not feedback. "The title is hard to read on mobile" is.
- Unfinished work is welcome here. That is the whole point of this place.

## How we behave

- Be kind and be patient — everyone here was a total beginner recently.
- Questions are always welcome. Asking is a builder's skill, not a weakness.
- When someone ships, celebrate loudly.

## Not allowed

- Mocking, insults, or harsh sarcasm — even as a "joke."
- Sharing anyone's personal information.
- Sharing passwords, API keys, or invite links outside the club.

## Enforcement

- First time: a private reminder from the instructor.
- Second time: you're out. No debates.
- See something wrong? Tell the instructor. Reporting protects the club.

The instructor is the product's only moderator. The instructor may hide or
restore content but does not rewrite member content. See the
[product definition](docs/PRODUCT.md) for permissions and the
[workflows](docs/WORKFLOWS.md) for moderation behavior.
